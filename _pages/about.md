<p class="home-lead">I am an undergraduate researcher interested in building robot learning systems that are geometrically grounded, experimentally rigorous, and useful in the physical world.</p>

## Current Role

I am a **Research Intern at [Noematrix](https://www.noematrix.ai/en)**, an embodied AI company developing general-purpose robot intelligence. My work focuses on deploying and evaluating vision-language-action and world-action-model policies on robotic manipulators, together with calibration, SLAM, teleoperation, and closed-loop evaluation workflows.

## Education

I am pursuing a B.S. in **Applied Mathematics & Computational Sciences / Computer Science** at **Duke Kunshan University and Duke University**, Class of 2028.

## Research Interests

- Robot learning and embodied AI
- Geometric deep learning and equivariant neural networks
- Diffusion, vision-language-action, and world-action models
- Simulation evaluation and real-robot deployment
