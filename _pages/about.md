<p class="home-lead">I am an undergraduate researcher interested in developing innovative and experimental robot policies and systems that are useful in the physical world.</p>

## Current Role

I am a **Research Intern at [Noematrix](https://www.noematrix.ai/en)**, an embodied AI company developing general-purpose robot intelligence. My work focuses on deploying robot policies on physical robot platforms, hand-eye calibration, SLAM accuracy evaluation, teleoperation, and closed-loop benchmarking workflows.

## Education

I am pursuing a B.S. in **Applied Mathematics & Computational Sciences / Computer Science** at **Duke Kunshan University and Duke University**, Class of 2028.

## Research Interests

- Robot Learning and Embodied AI
- Equivariant Neural Networks
- Deep Learning and Computer Vision
