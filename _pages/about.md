<p class="home-lead home-lead--justified">I am an undergraduate researcher interested in developing innovative, experimentally grounded robot learning methods and systems for real-world deployment.</p>

## Current Role

I am a **Research Intern at [Noematrix](https://www.noematrix.ai/en)**, an embodied AI company developing general-purpose robot intelligence. My work focuses on deploying robot policies on physical robot platforms, hand-eye calibration, SLAM accuracy evaluation, building teleoperation platform, and closed-loop benchmarking workflows.

## Education

I am pursuing a B.S. in **Applied Mathematics & Computational Sciences / Computer Science** at **Duke Kunshan University and Duke University**, Class of 2028.
