# Repository Agent Instructions

## Bilingual Content Policy

1. Treat `_pages/about.md`, the English homepage, as the canonical source of truth for homepage content.
2. Keep `_pages/zh.md` as a faithful Chinese translation of the English page. Match its section order, claims, entries, links, emphasis, publication status, and level of detail.
3. Make content decisions in English first, then update the corresponding Chinese translation in the same change.
4. Do not add Chinese-only claims, sections, list items, or status information. If the two pages conflict, preserve the English content and bring the Chinese page back into alignment.
